Privacy Policy

Junk Cleaner: File Manager

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Junk Cleaner: File Manager ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal files, file contents, photos, videos, documents, or any data about the specific files or folders on your device to any server. All file scanning, junk detection, and cleaning happens entirely on your device. We have no access to your files.

2. Permissions We Use and Why

  • All Files Access / Storage Access: Used only to scan your device's storage locally to identify junk files (cache, temporary files, residual files, large files, old downloads) so you can review and delete them. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used only to read per-app storage/cache size and app-open history, shown to you inside the App so you can decide which apps to clear cache for or uninstall. This information is read locally and is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Delete Packages / Uninstall: Used only to let you trigger Android's own system uninstall confirmation dialog for an app you choose. We cannot uninstall anything without your explicit confirmation on Android's own screen.

3. Advertising

The current version of the App does not display any ads and does not use any advertising SDKs. If ads are introduced in a future update, this policy will be updated in advance to describe what advertising partners are used and what data they may collect (such as device identifiers for ad delivery and measurement), along with any required consent options.

4. No Account, No Personal Data Collection

The App does not require account creation, login, or collection of personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
